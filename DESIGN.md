# Design Tokens — Google Stitch Layout

## 1. Color Palette
- **Background Deep:** `#0a0a0f` (Rich obsidian backdrop)
- **Surface Dark:** `#161622` (Card and modal panels)
- **Primary Accent:** `#6366f1` (Indigo action buttons & active states)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#ec4899` (Pink highlights and badges)
- **Text Main:** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50)
- **Text Muted:** `#94a3b8` (Slate 400)
- **Success / Flash:** `#ffffff` (Pure white overlay flash)

## 2. Typography
- **Font Family:** system-ui, -apple-system, 'SF Pro Display', sans-serif
- **Headings:** 700 weight, tight line-height
- **Body:** 400/500 weight, readable high contrast

## 3. Spacing & Touch Targets
- **Minimum Tap Height:** 72px for all primary action buttons
- **Border Radius:** 24px for containers, 16px for interactive cards
- **Padding/Margins:** 16px to 32px grid scale
